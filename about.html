About Electric City Baptist Church

Everything for Christ,
by Christ

A church planted in 2015 to make Jesus famous in Schenectady, the Electric City, and around the world.

Our Mission

It's not about us.
It's all about Him.

So many have broken lives and are going in an aimless direction, while others have replaced their need for Jesus with empty pursuits and unfulfilling goals.

“Everything we do from the time we wake up till we go to sleep should be wrapped around this statement: Everything for Christ, by Christ.”

We know there is none other worthy of our pursuits and praise than the Lord Christ Jesus. Through the Word of God we can bring this message to Schenectady and make Jesus famous in this town and around the world.

Meet the Staffords

Our Pastor

Meet the
Stafford Family

Pastor Anthony trained under the ministries of Lancaster Baptist Church and West Coast Baptist College, which is where he met his wife, Bethany.

After their time in California they were married and moved to Schenectady, NY in 2011, where they served as Children's Ministry leaders at Lighthouse Baptist Church under Pastor John Horn. In 2015 they began to pray and talk about planting a church in Schenectady. After counsel and guidance, Anthony and Bethany, along with their two children, Lincoln and Penelope, moved forward with God's plan for their lives.
